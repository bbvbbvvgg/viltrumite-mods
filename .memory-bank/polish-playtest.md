# Engine facts found in the general-polish playtest (PR #87)
- Forge `RenderLevelStageEvent` AFTER_LEVEL: `getPoseStack()` is the bare projection, not the camera view. World-space VFX at that stage must rebuild the view (`ZP roll · XP pitch · YP yaw+180`, `ScreenProjector.viewRotation`); `PixelVfx.levelView` does this. AFTER_PARTICLES and earlier stages still carry the view in the pose.
- Vanilla `HumanoidModel.setupAnim` never resets `head.zRot` / `hat.zRot` / `body.zRot`, and `PlayerModel` is shared by every player. A poser that writes `+=` to them accumulates every frame and leaks to other players: assign, never add. `PlayerModelNeckResetMixin` zeroes them at HEAD of `setupAnim`.
- `HeroDamage.applyCleanDamage` fires no Forge event, so `HeroDefinition.onHurt` never sees those hits. Use `onAttacked` / `onAttacking` (dispatched by `HeroDamageLayers.notifyAttack` from LivingAttackEvent and the clean path, absorbed hits included) for "who hit whom" memory.
- Reinhard World Erasure: the cinematic (black screen) starts `ReinhardRules.ERASE_SPEECH_TICKS` after the key press; the world is paused for speech + `ERASE_LENGTH`, and the subtitle `ReinhardLines.WORLD_ERASURE` (3 pages) must end before the dark phase in every locale (`HeroLinesTest`).
