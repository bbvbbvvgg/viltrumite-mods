- Ruling: a cleanup (death, logout, hero change) resets Reid to SHEATHED at once (`ReidToggle.cancel` with a reason); a control on Reinhard or a dimension change only cuts a running draw short (DRAWN stays drawn) — cost if wrong: a frozen Reinhard keeps Reid in his hand.
- Ruling: a DRAWN Reid is saved with its idle ticks (relog keeps the sword and the bar); DRAWING / SHEATHING load as SHEATHED (plan); a fresh body (`respawnCopy`) starts SHEATHED — cost if wrong: none.
- Ruling: `SHIPPED_STAGE = 4` from Task 2 (plan) ships the Holy Sword slots 1–4 at once: Sheath Strike and Rain of Cuts sit in the loadout with the router's refusal (no handler) until Tasks 7–8 add them, their names / icons arrive with them — cost if wrong: an intermediate commit shows two unfinished slots.

- Ruling (Task 3): the lift rescans every 5 t (REID_LIFT_SCAN_INTERVAL) within 96 blocks; between scans the tracked liftees are stepped every tick — cost if wrong: entities entering the radius join up to 5 t late.
- Ruling (Task 3): the server zeroes lifted entities' horizontal velocity and sets the vertical one without hurtMarked (players get it through the regular motion sync) — cost if wrong: lifted players drift slightly on the client.
- Ruling (Task 3): controlled entities and entities that refuse impulses (HeroRegistry.allowsImpulse / HeroImpulse) are skipped; ControlManager stores the natural noGravity from LiftLeases so a lift and a control never fight over the flag — cost if wrong: a controlled target stays on the ground during the draw.
- Ruling (Task 3): LiftLeases mirrors every lease in an entity tag (owners + baseY + natural noGravity); orphan tags are restored on EntityJoinLevel, all leases are cleared on ServerStopped — cost if wrong: an entity unloaded mid-lift could keep noGravity until it reloads (then restored).
- Ruling (Task 3): overlapping lifts of two casters cap at lease baseY + REID_LIFT_CAP (3.0) — cost if wrong: two simultaneous draws lift less than 2×.
- Ruling (Task 3): the final wave pushes only impulse-allowing living entities within 24 blocks (armor stands excluded), no damage, knockback falls linearly to 0 at the edge — cost if wrong: decorative entities stay in place.
- Ruling (Task 3): the far sound is sent to every player on the server in every dimension, AMBIENT category, volume 0.4, at the listener's own position; the chat line goes to every player — cost if wrong: players in other dimensions hear it too (spec says "the whole server").
- Ruling (Task 3): the scene packet START is resent every 20 t to all players within 96 blocks (late joiners pick it up); the caster always receives START/END — cost if wrong: one extra small packet per second per nearby player.
- Ruling (Task 3): a control effect or dimension change during the draw cuts it short (sheathe, lift ends, END sent); jump and air step are refused while drawing and the client clears movement input — cost if wrong: the draw cannot be cancelled by moving.
