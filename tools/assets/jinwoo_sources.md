# Sung Jinwoo asset sources

One row per committed asset group. Every Jinwoo asset is **own work** generated by scripts in this repository (Notion AI for grebeshok105), released under CC0-1.0. No external recordings, samples, textures or models were downloaded or copied; no vanilla Minecraft sounds or particles are referenced.

## Sounds

All sounds are original deterministic synthesis by `tools/sfx/jinwoo.py` (numpy: seeded noise, oscillators, filters, convolution tail; no recording, no download). Packaged as mono 44.1 kHz Vorbis by `.agents/skills/sound-design/scripts/openverse_sfx.py package --own`. Regenerate: `python3 tools/sfx/jinwoo.py --package`. Not auditioned on studio monitors; final in-game balance still needs a listening pass.
