# T-1000: The Infiltrator — design

Status: implementation in `feat/t1000-infiltrator` (MAX session 3). Core mod `viltrumitecore` 1.71.0.

## 1. What it is

An extremely rare autonomous NPC of the Atheria city. It lives **as one of the citizens**: the same
`CitizenEntity` code path, the same `CitizenLook` seed, the same `CitizenRenderer`, the same walking and
idle animations. No glow, no particles, no boss bar, no title, no music, no name tag differences. A player can
only tell it from a real citizen by its behaviour: it keeps showing up behind them, it stops when they turn
around, it never panics with the crowd once it has chosen a target.

When it attacks it turns into polished liquid metal: a procedural chrome body (own CPU mesh, matcap shading,
smooth reveal from the first wound outwards), arms that slowly stretch into blades, hooks or spikes, bullet
holes that close, a head that splits and flows back, droplets that tear off and crawl home, a fall into a
puddle and a rise out of it.

Shared seams added to the city code (small, opt-in, default behaviour unchanged):
`CitizenEntity#citizenMind()` (crowd reactions on/off), `#discardWhenStuck()`, `#lostOffGrid()` (the walk-grid
cleanup: citizens are discarded, the T-1000 turns dormant), `#cityTracked()`, `#capturable()`,
protected `setCitizenState`, `Bystander#isBystander()` (a revealed T-1000 is a valid hero target),
`CitizenRenderer`/`CitizenModel` open for subclassing, and a rare **police uniform** top for ordinary citizens
(so a policeman in the crowd is normal and the T-1000 may wear one too).

## 3. Lifecycle and persistence

* The entity type is `noSave`: it is never written into a chunk and never keeps one loaded.
* `T1000SavedData` (overworld storage, `data/viltrumitecore_t1000.dat`) remembers every instance:
  instance id, disguise, integrity/core, last position, `dormant` flag and since when.
* Chunk unload / server stop / long time far from every player / lost off the walk grid while unobserved →
  the record turns **dormant** (never "destroyed": only a real death or an erasure by something else counts).
  After a delay the scheduler re-materialises it (one per check) near a player inside the city, out of every
  camera, in an already ticking chunk. It "followed" them. Nothing respawns instantly, nothing in bulk.
* After a restart all records are dormant; a startup grace keeps them away for the first minutes.
* Final destruction removes the record and starts the respawn delay (`t1000RespawnDelayMinutes`).
* The city population manager never sees it (not in `CityManager.citizens()`), so density trims and
  citizen cleanup never remove it.

## 4. Rare scheduler

Every `t1000NaturalCheckSeconds` (default 300 s) one roll of `t1000NaturalChancePercent` (default 1.5 %)
— on average one appearance every ~5.5 hours of play inside the city — and only when: natural spawn on, a city
profile exists and is enabled, difficulty not peaceful, a player stands in the arena, active instances below
`t1000MaxActive` (default 1), the respawn delay after the last destruction is over, at least a few citizens are
around the chosen site (it needs a crowd to hide in), the site is a pedestrian cell 36–64 blocks away, in an
entity-ticking chunk, behind every camera (view cone + line-of-sight test, as SCP-096).

## 5. AI

States (server, one `T1000Hunt` goal over the citizen goals; the citizen goals run while disguised):

1. **DISGUISE** – a normal citizen; crowd reactions on. After a calm period it picks a target (players in the
   city, nearest first, never spectators/creative).
2. **SEARCH** – walks (citizen pace, citizen navigation) to a point behind the target; repaths every 2 s
   through the shared `PathBudget`; no per-tick global pathfinding.
3. **STALK** – keeps 7–14 blocks, prefers being behind; when the target looks at it, it stops, looks away,
   pretends to idle or turns into a side street. A suspicion meter rises while it is watched; it never runs.
4. **REVEAL** – triggered by an opportunity (target isolated/back turned/close/indoors/hurt), by being
   stared down, by being hurt, or by the stalk timer. Chrome spreads from the chest, the head tilts, one arm
   slowly becomes a blade; the city danger map learns about it (the crowd flees).
5. **CHASE** – door-capable ground navigation, run speed close to a sprinting player, leaps over gaps, flows
   through thin obstacles (fences, bars, panes, doors: at most two blocks), last-known-position search,
   gives up after ~30 s without contact.
6. **RECOVER** – integrity 0: melts into a puddle, crawls, reforms after a few seconds.
7. **CONCEAL** – after losing the target: walks out of sight, copies a nearby citizen it is not observed with
   (the original stays), flows back into human form, returns to DISGUISE with a long cooldown.

Attacks: fast stab, sweeping slash, hook pull, grab-and-throw, counter (parry + riposte when struck in the
face of a ready blade), all with cooldowns and wind-ups readable in the animation.

## 6. Damage model

Two pools: **integrity** (the liquid shell, absorbs ordinary damage, regenerates after a short delay) and
**core** (the vanilla health). Ordinary hits bleed 15 % into the core; heavy hits and explosions bleed more and
deform the body; fire and heat bypass the shell and stop regeneration; freezing (powder snow, frost effects,
cold damage types) makes it brittle — the next heavy hit shatters it. Integrity 0 = puddle (vulnerable).
Core 0 = real final destruction (long disintegration, no loot, no XP). Not one-shot: one hit never takes more
than a share of the core unless the body is frozen or already a puddle. No item drops, nothing to farm.

## 7. Sound

Own CC0 samples (Openverse/Freesound), processed into OGG cues by `tools/sfx/t1000.py`; sources and CC0 proof in
`tools/assets/t1000_sources.md`, shipped in the jar under `META-INF/credits/`. In disguise it sounds like a
citizen (vanilla player hurt/steps); metal sounds only after the reveal.

## 8. Commands

`/t1000 status | spawn [pos] | remove | natural on|off|check|force | config chance|delay|interval|max <v> |
debug reveal|conceal|puddle|destroy` — level 2. Messages never contain the position of a hidden instance.
